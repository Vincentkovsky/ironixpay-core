## 4. Payment Services & Asset Flow

### 4.1 Service Description

The Platform provides merchants with stablecoin (USDT/USDC) collection, automated sweeping, and withdrawal services.

### 4.2 Asset Flow Disclosure

When a customer sends stablecoin (USDT or USDC) to a merchant's payment address:

1. **Collection**: Funds arrive at a blockchain address derived and managed by the Platform on behalf of the merchant.
2. **Sweeping**: The Platform automatically transfers received funds from the collection address to the Platform's treasury address. This is a routine operational process required for settlement.
3. **Settlement**: After deducting applicable fees, the net amount is credited to the merchant's balance within the Platform.
4. **Withdrawal**: Merchants may withdraw their available balance to an external blockchain address at any time, subject to applicable fees and security verification.

Merchants acknowledge that funds do not remain at the original collection address after sweeping and that balances displayed in the Platform are accounting entries backed by the Platform's treasury reserves.

### 4.3 Transaction Finality

- Payment amounts are determined by the actual stablecoin amount confirmed on-chain.
- Transactions are irreversible once confirmed on the blockchain.
- Service fees are charged at published rates. Rate changes will be communicated at least 14 days in advance.
- The Platform does not handle fiat currencies. All transactions involve on-chain crypto assets only.
